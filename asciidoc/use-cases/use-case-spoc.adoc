== Use Case Feature {var_use_case_id}: <<label_use_case_name_spoc>> (<<acronym_spoc>>)


=== Narrative

In the ICU, Nurse Maya is monitoring Mr. Alvarez, who is mechanically ventilated and connected to several infusion pumps and a patient monitor. While there is no clinician in the ICU, the device alarms are coordinated through a Distributed Alarm System (DAS) and made available at the central station, allowing Maya to monitor them without being in the room. Beyond announcing alarms (though an ICU Dashboard or an Alert Annunciation Service), the central station also coordinates the Silent Point of Care (SPoC). To reduce noise around Mr. Alvarez, Maya activates Silent PoC mode at the central station. Consequently, the devices mute their local auditory alarm signals while alarms continue to be announced through the central station.

=== Benefits

By muting auditory alarms at the bedside while retaining central annunciation, Silent PoC can reduce disruptive auditory input around the patient. This may help support sleep and a stable day–night rhythm, lower stress and anxiety, reduce the risk of delirium, and potentially support faster recovery.

For clinical users, remote annunciation of alarms at the clinician's location can help them review and prioritize alarm conditions across patients without first visiting each patient room. This may improve alarm visibility and management, reduce workload and mental fatigue, and support communication among the care team.

[role=use-case-scenario,sdpi_scenario="Place a PoC in Silent Mode - No Active Alarms"]
[oid-arcs=".2"]
==== Scenario: <<acronym_spoc>> {var_use_case_id}.2 - Place a PoC in Silent Mode - No Active Alarms

[role=use-case-steps]
====
*Given* A DAS has been established, and Silent PoC mode is allowed at the device(s)

*Given* The device(s) has / have NOT not detected an alarm condition

*When* The clinical user initiates Silent PoC mode at the SPoC Coordinator  

*Then* The device(s) enable(s) Silent PoC mode, thereby muting local annunciation of auditory alarm signals

*When* An alarm is detected at the device(s)

*Then* The SPoC Coordinator annunciates the auditory alarm signal through an Alert Annunciation Service

*Then* The device(s) remains quiet
====

[role=use-case-scenario,sdpi_scenario="Silent PoC with local deactivation and manual re-activation"]
[oid-arcs=".9"]
==== Scenario: <<acronym_spoc>> {var_use_case_id}.9 - Silent PoC with local deactivation and manual re-activation

[role=use-case-steps]
====
*Given* A DAS has been established and placed in Silent PoC mode

*When* A clinical user locally deactivates Silent PoC mode at a device (so as to hear any device alarm signals while being at the patient's PoC)

*Then* The device exits Silent PoC mode and resumes local alarm annunciation

*When* The clinical user locally re-activates Silent PoC mode at the device (before leaving the patient's PoC)

*Then* The device re-enters Silent PoC mode, suspends local alarm annunciation and notifies the clinical user of the Silent PoC mode re-activation
====

[role=use-case-scenario,sdpi_scenario="Silent PoC with local deactivation and automatic re-activation"]

[role=use-case-steps]
====
*Given* A DAS has been established and placed in Silent PoC mode

*When* A clinical user locally deactivates Silent PoC mode at a device (so as to hear any device alarm signals while being at the patient's PoC)

*Then* The device exits Silent PoC mode and resumes local alarm annunciation

*Then* After a preset period of time, the device automatically re-enters Silent PoC mode, suspends local alarm annunciation and notifies the clinical user of the Silent PoC mode re-activation
